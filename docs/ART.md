# Art provenance

The campaign uses two original ImageGen outputs generated for this project. No web-downloaded art or third-party game sprite packs are included.

- `Assets/Resources/Medieval/warriors.png`: transparent RGBA, 948 × 1659; seven character types and four poses each.
- `Assets/Resources/Medieval/props.png`: transparent RGBA, 1254 × 1254; sixteen environment objects.

The PNG files are the unedited generator outputs. `MedievalArt.cs` defines explicit source rectangles because generated spacing is not an exact grid. Unity slices the atlases at runtime and aligns feet/body pivots. Movement alternates two walk poses; attacks use a fourth pose. Sprites face left/right by mirroring, not eight-direction animation. The terrain and combat markers are generated by C#; sound is synthesized locally.

These are fictional, historically inspired characters, not validated reconstructions. The Templar is an independent order ally, not a playable country.

## Warrior prompt

Production sprite atlas for a medieval low-fantasy top-down action RPG. Transparent RGBA background. EXACT grid: FOUR columns and SEVEN rows, 28 equal cells, portrait canvas aspect 4:7, ideally1024x1792. No lines, no labels, no text. Each cell contains ONE complete centered character, same scale, feet aligned, 20% clear padding, no character overlaps its cell. Cohesive crisp pixel art, earthy colors and dark outlines, overhead three-quarter camera facing down/right, approximately48pixel character detail. Row1 Rus druzhina warrior red cloak conical iron helmet chainmail spear and round shield. Row2 French knight blue surcoat great helm sword and kite shield. Row3 Norwegian green tunic fur shoulders nasal helmet NO horns, axe and round shield. Row4 brigand brown hood leather short sword buckler. Row5 archer charcoal hood dark red tunic bow quiver. Row6 enemy captain dark chainmail burgundy cloak heavy mace. Row7 Templar white tabard red cross mail simple helmet sword and shield. Within EVERY row keep exactly the same character and equipment: column1 idle, column2 walking left leg forward, column3 walking right leg forward, column4 attacking (archer draws bow). All weapons entirely inside cells. All28sprites fullbody isolated on real transparency. No ground or scenery, no checkerboard, no borders, no lettering, no watermarks. Restrained medieval realism stylized into actual readable game sprites.

## Environment prompt

Production environment sprite atlas for medieval top-down low-fantasy RPG. Exact FOUR by FOUR grid on a square canvas, 16 equal square cells, one isolated prop centered per cell, actual transparent RGBA background, no text/gridlines. Cohesive hand-painted pixel art with crisp dark outlines and detailed earthy wood/stone/foliage, overhead three-quarter view; each prop fits fully within its cell with at least12% transparent padding. Reading order row1: small timber cottage with straw roof; stone medieval gatehouse with wooden arched doors; square crenellated stone tower; tall pine tree. Row2: leafy oak tree; wooden merchant wagon loaded with sacks; circular stone well with small timber roof; group of wooden barrels. Row3: wooden crates; lit iron brazier with restrained orange flame; mossy boulder; old stone grave marker. Row4: triangular campaign tent with red cloth; short horizontal wooden palisade section; short horizontal crenellated stone wall; small ruined stone arch. No people, no lettering, no floor tiles under objects, no scenery beyond object silhouettes, no watermarks. Consistent scale and lighting, suitable to place as decorative props in actual2D Unity battle maps. Authentic medieval feeling, modest pixelart not glossy 3D. Keep props distinct and do not cross cell boundaries.
